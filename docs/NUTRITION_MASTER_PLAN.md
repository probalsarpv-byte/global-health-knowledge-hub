## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

The **Functional Nutrition & Dietetics Academy** establishes a world-class, rigorous, bilingual (English and Bengali) learning experience bridging the biochemical mechanisms of human digestion, cellular energetics, micronutrient coenzymology, gut microbiome metagenomics, and clinical Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT).

Building upon the successful architectures of the **Anatomy Academy** (28 chapters) and **Human Metabolism Academy** (36 chapters), this Academy offers a fully integrated, accessible, and evidence-based curriculum designed for medical students, dietetics interns, clinical nutritionists, biomedical researchers, and global health learners.

## 2. 26-Chapter Curriculum Taxonomy

The curriculum is organized into 6 coherent, logically ascending thematic groups:

### Group 1: Foundations of Human Nutrition & Energetics (Chapters 01–04)
- **Chapter 01: Energy Balance, Basal Metabolic Rate & Total Daily Energy Expenditure**  
  *Slug:* `energy-balance-tdee`  
  *Scope:* First law of thermodynamics in human biology, direct/indirect calorimetry, respiratory quotient (RQ), components of TDEE (BMR/RMR, TEF, NEAT, EAT), Schofield and Mifflin-St Jeor equations, and metabolic adaptation.
- **Chapter 02: Macronutrient Digestion, Brush-Border Transport & Absorption Mechanics**  
  *Slug:* `macronutrient-digestion`  
  *Scope:* Mechanical and chemical digestion across GI tract, luminal zymogens, brush-border hydrolases, enterocyte apical/basolateral transport kinetics (SGLT1, GLUT5, GLUT2, PepT1, FAT/CD36), and chylomicron versus portal partitioning.
- **Chapter 03: Carbohydrate Quality: Glycemic Index, Glycemic Load & Resistant Starch**  
  *Slug:* `carbohydrate-quality-gi-gl`  
  *Scope:* Monosaccharides, disaccharides, and polysaccharides; amylose vs amylopectin structure; enzymatic digestion rates; standardized GI/GL testing; types 1–4 resistant starches; postprandial glucose-insulin dynamics.
- **Chapter 04: Dietary Fiber, Short-Chain Fatty Acids & Colonic Epithelial Integrity**  
  *Slug:* `dietary-fiber-scfa`  
  *Scope:* Soluble vs insoluble dietary fibers, viscosity and gel formation, colonic microbial fermentation, acetate/propionate/butyrate kinetics, FFAR2/FFAR3 receptor signaling, and colonic colonocyte beta-oxidation.

### Group 2: Lipid & Protein Nutrition (Chapters 05–08)
- **Chapter 05: Dietary Lipids: Fatty Acid Saturation, Trans-Fats & Essential PUFA Ratios**  
  *Slug:* `dietary-lipids-fats-pufa`  
  *Scope:* Saturated, monounsaturated, and polyunsaturated fatty acid structures; industrial vs ruminant trans-fats; linoleic acid (Omega-6) and alpha-linolenic acid (Omega-3); desaturase/elongase competition; eicosanoid and docosanoid inflammatory mediators.
- **Chapter 06: Dietary Proteins, Amino Acid Quality, DIAAS/PDCAAS & Nitrogen Balance**  
  *Slug:* `dietary-proteins-amino-acids`  
  *Scope:* Essential vs conditionally essential amino acids, protein turnover, whole-body nitrogen balance, protein quality scoring systems (PDCAAS vs DIAAS), ileal amino acid digestibility, and muscle protein synthesis (MPS) leucine threshold.
- **Chapter 07: Satiety Neuroendocrinology: Leptin, Ghrelin, GLP-1, PYY & Appetite Regulation**  
  *Slug:* `satiety-neuroendocrinology`  
  *Scope:* Arcuate nucleus circuitry (NPY/AgRP orexigenic vs POMC/CART anorexigenic neurons), gastric ghrelin pulsatility, enteroendocrine L-cell GLP-1/PYY secretion, CCK vagal afferents, and leptin adipostat signaling.
- **Chapter 08: Cellular Hydration, Fluid Balance & Electrolyte Osmoregulation**  
  *Slug:* `hydration-fluid-electrolytes`  
  *Scope:* Intracellular vs extracellular fluid compartments, Starling forces, thirst threshold, hypothalamic osmoreceptors, vasopressin (AVP/ADH) aquaporin-2 translocation, and renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS).

### Group 3: Micronutrients, Bioactives & Cellular Coenzymes (Chapters 09–13)
- **Chapter 09: Fat-Soluble Vitamins (A, D, E, K): Genomic Signaling & Physiology**  
  *Slug:* `fat-soluble-vitamins`  
  *Scope:* Retinol/retinal/retinoic acid (RAR/RXR signaling, visual cycle); cholecalciferol-calcitriol axis (VDR, PTH crosstalk); alpha-tocopherol lipid antioxidant chain-breaking; phylloquinone/menaquinone (VKOR, gamma-glutamyl carboxylase, osteocalcin, clotting factors).
- **Chapter 10: Water-Soluble Vitamins: B-Complex Coenzymes in Mitochondrial Bioenergetics**  
  *Slug:* `b-complex-vitamins-bioenergetics`  
  *Scope:* Thiamine (TPP, pyruvate dehydrogenase, beriberi); Riboflavin (FMN/FAD, Complex I/II); Niacin (NAD/NADP, pellagra); Pantothenic acid (CoA, ACP); Pyridoxine (PLP, transamination, decarboxylation); Biotin (carboxylases); Cobalamin & Folate (one-carbon metabolism).
- **Chapter 11: Vitamin C, Trace Minerals & Cellular Antioxidant Defense Networks**  
  *Slug:* `vitamin-c-trace-minerals-redox`  
  *Scope:* Ascorbic acid prolyl hydroxylase cofactor and collagen synthesis; superoxide dismutases (Cu/Zn-SOD, Mn-SOD); glutathione peroxidase (selenium); catalase (iron); zinc finger transcription factors; Fenton chemistry.
- **Chapter 12: Macrominerals & Bone Matrix: Calcium, Phosphorus, Magnesium Homeostasis**  
  *Slug:* `macrominerals-bone-calcium-magnesium`  
  *Scope:* Hydroxyapatite crystal structure; dynamic bone remodeling (osteoblasts/osteoclasts); calcium sensing receptor (CaSR); parathyroid hormone, calcitriol, and calcitonin; intracellular magnesium-ATP chelation.
- **Chapter 13: Iron Biology: Dietary Bioavailability, Ferritin, Transferrin & Hepcidin**  
  *Slug:* `iron-biology-hepcidin-anemia`  
  *Scope:* Heme vs non-heme dietary iron; apical DMT1 and Dcytb; basolateral ferroportin and hephaestin; circulating transferrin saturation; ferritin cellular storage; hepcidin systemic master-regulation in inflammation and iron overload.

### Group 4: Gastrointestinal Physiology, Microbiome & Gut Barrier (Chapters 14–17)
- **Chapter 14: The Gastrointestinal Barrier: Mucosal Immunity & Tight Junction Integrity**  
  *Slug:* `gut-barrier-mucosal-immunity`  
  *Scope:* Enterocyte apical brush border, claudin/occludin/ZO-1 tight junction complexes; mucin layer (MUC2); gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT), Peyer's patches, secretory IgA (sIgA) transcytosis; toll-like receptors and LPS translocation.
- **Chapter 15: The Human Gut Microbiome: Taxonomic Architecture & Metagenomics**  
  *Slug:* `gut-microbiome-metagenomics`  
  *Scope:* Dominant phyla (Bacillota/Firmicutes, Bacteroidota/Bacteroidetes, Actinomycetota, Pseudomonadota); alpha and beta diversity; metagenomic functional capacity; microbial biotransformation of polyphenols, bile acids (secondary bile acids), and xenobiotics.
- **Chapter 16: Prebiotics, Probiotics, Synbiotics & Dietary Interventions in Dysbiosis**  
  *Slug:* `prebiotics-probiotics-dysbiosis`  
  *Scope:* Inulin, fructooligosaccharides (FOS), galactooligosaccharides (GOS); verified probiotic strains (Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium, Saccharomyces boulardii); mechanisms of pathogen competitive exclusion; clinical indications and antibiotic-associated diarrhea.
- **Chapter 17: Food Intolerances, Non-Celiac Gluten Sensitivity, Celiac Disease & Food Allergies**  
  *Slug:* `food-intolerances-celiac-allergy`  
  *Scope:* Primary lactase non-persistence vs secondary hypolactasia; hereditary fructose intolerance; IgE-mediated food allergy (anaphylaxis, mast cell degranulation); Celiac disease autoantigen transglutaminase 2 (tTG) and HLA-DQ2/DQ8; NCGS diagnostic boundaries.

### Group 5: Clinical Nutrition, Metabolic Health & Medical Nutrition Therapy (Chapters 18–22)
- **Chapter 18: Nutritional Management of Obesity, Insulin Resistance & Type 2 Diabetes**  
  *Slug:* `nutrition-obesity-t2d-mnt`  
  *Scope:* Adipose tissue dysfunction, ectopic lipid deposition; hyperinsulinemic euglycemic clamp physiology; Medical Nutrition Therapy for glycemic control (ADA/EASD consensus); carbohydrate counting, meal sequencing, energy restriction, and weight loss remission.
- **Chapter 19: Cardiovascular Dietetics: Atherosclerosis, Dyslipidemia & Hypertension**  
  *Slug:* `cardiovascular-dietetics-dash-mediterranean`  
  *Scope:* LDL particle atherogenicity and apoB; dietary fatty acids effect on hepatic LDL receptor expression; DASH diet trial mechanisms (sodium restriction, potassium/magnesium augmentation); Mediterranean diet PREDIMED trial evidence; phytosterols.
- **Chapter 20: Renal Nutrition: Chronic Kidney Disease & Glomerular Filtration**  
  *Slug:* `renal-nutrition-ckd-mnt`  
  *Scope:* Progressive CKD stages and glomerular hyperfiltration; dietary protein restriction and nitrogenous waste; phosphorus binders and FGF23-Klotho axis; potassium management in hyperkalemia; fluid management in end-stage renal disease.
- **Chapter 21: Hepatobiliary & Gastrointestinal Disorders: MASLD/MASH, IBD & Low-FODMAP**  
  *Slug:* `gi-liver-nutrition-masld-ibd-fodmap`  
  *Scope:* Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD); fructose hepatic lipogenesis; inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's, Ulcerative Colitis) malnutrition risks; irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) luminal distension and low-FODMAP intervention protocol.
- **Chapter 22: Clinical Malnutrition, Protein-Energy Wasting & Nutritional Support**  
  *Slug:* `clinical-malnutrition-enteral-parenteral`  
  *Scope:* GLIM diagnostic criteria for malnutrition; marasmus vs kwashiorkor; cachexia vs sarcopenia; refeeding syndrome pathophysiology (hypophosphatemia, cardiac arrhythmias); indications for enteral tube feeding vs total parenteral nutrition (TPN).

### Group 6: Lifespan, Sports & Functional Precision Dietetics (Chapters 23–26)
- **Chapter 23: Maternal, Fetal & Lactational Nutrition: Embryogenesis to Breastfeeding**  
  *Slug:* `maternal-fetal-lactation-nutrition`  
  *Scope:* Preconception folate and neural tube defect prevention; placental nutrient transport; gestational diabetes screening and nutrition; iron and choline requirements in fetal brain development; human milk oligosaccharides (HMOs) and lactogenesis biochemistry.
- **Chapter 24: Pediatric, Adolescent & Geriatric Nutrition: Growth to Sarcopenia**  
  *Slug:* `lifespan-pediatric-geriatric-nutrition`  
  *Scope:* WHO growth charts and stunting/wasting; complementary feeding transition; adolescent peak bone mass accretion; geriatric anorexia of aging, anabolic resistance, atrophic gastritis, and vitamin B12 malabsorption.
- **Chapter 25: Exercise Nutrition & Sports Bioenergetics: Periodization & Timing**  
  *Slug:* `sports-nutrition-bioenergetics`  
  *Scope:* Fuel selection across exercise intensities (crossover concept); glycogen supercompensation protocols; intra-workout carbohydrate hydrogels; post-exercise glycogen resynthesis and muscle protein synthesis; evidence-based ergogenic aids (creatine, caffeine, beta-alanine).
- **Chapter 26: Nutrigenomics, Epigenetics & Precision Functional Dietetics**  
  *Slug:* `nutrigenomics-precision-dietetics`  
  *Scope:* Single nucleotide polymorphisms (MTHFR C677T, APOE epsilon-4, TCF7L2, CYP1A2 caffeine metabolism); DNA methylation and histone acetylation via dietary methyl donors (choline, betaine, folate); promise and limitations of commercial direct-to-consumer nutrigenetic testing.
