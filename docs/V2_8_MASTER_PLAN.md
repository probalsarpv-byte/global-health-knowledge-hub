## 1. Executive Vision: Medical Nutrition Therapy in Action

The Global Health Knowledge Hub separates pure mechanistic science from applied clinical practice:
- **Volume I: Functional Nutrition & Human Nutritional Biochemistry (Chapters 01–27):**  
  Covers the cellular, molecular, and organ-system mechanisms (apical enterocyte transporters, reverse cholesterol transport, hepatic de novo lipogenesis, mitochondrial beta-oxidation, DIAAS protein kinetics, and micronutrient coenzymes).
- **Volume II: Practical Diet Planning & Therapeutic Dietetics (Chapters 01–32):**  
  Equips medical students, dietetic interns, and healthcare professionals to assess patients, formulate individualized nutritional prescriptions, translate nutrient grams into culturally adapted meals, and administer disease-specific Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT).
