## 1. Educational Philosophy: The Independent Textbook Standard

A chapter must teach the subject like a carefully written, comprehensive university textbook (such as *Modern Nutrition in Health and Disease*, *Krause's Food & the Nutrition Care Process*, or *Advanced Nutrition and Human Metabolism*), not a magazine summary, quick FAQ, or slide deck outline.

A student reading this platform must be able to study the chapter completely independently without needing to open Google to understand missing steps, undefined jargon, or abrupt conceptual leaps.

### Core Pedagogical Architecture (The 14-Point Topic Structure):
For every significant topic, where applicable, the author must provide:
1. **Clear Concept Introduction:** Grounding the topic in human biology before delving into technical terms.
2. **What It Is and Why It Matters:** Physiological necessity and consequences of insufficiency or excess.
3. **Relevant Biological Structures & Localization:** Anatomical organs, tissues, cell types, organelles, membranes, and cellular compartments.
4. **Mechanisms Explained Step by Step:** Sequential chronological actions (What happens first? What enzyme or transporter binds? What happens next? Where do the products go?).
5. **Detailed Molecular & Physiological Explanation:** Exact coenzymes, oxidation-reduction states, stoichiometric equations, receptor binding, and intracellular second messengers.
6. **Causes, Regulatory Factors & Influencing Conditions:** Endocrine regulation, substrate availability, allosteric activation/inhibition, pH, and physiological stress.
7. **Connection with Other Body Systems:** Integration across gastroenterology, endocrinology, nephrology, cardiology, immunology, and neurology.
8. **Meaningful Everyday Analogies & Examples:** Concrete physical metaphors to build intuition, explicitly distinguished from the actual molecular mechanism.
9. **Worked Numerical or Practical Examples:** Real stoichiometric calculations, energy expenditure formulas, nitrogen balance equations, or clinical case reasoning with explicit steps and conclusions.
10. **Nutritional, Clinical & Functional Relevance:** Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT), deficiency syndromes, toxicity risks, drug-nutrient interactions, and dietary guidelines.
11. **Important Evidence, Limitations & Exceptions:** Landmark clinical trials (e.g., PREDIMED, DiRECT, DASH, DPP), study design limits, and conflicting hypotheses.
12. **Relevant Diagrams & Visual Explanations:** Clean, labeled interactive diagrams linked directly to corresponding text sections.
13. **Concise Key Takeaways:** High-yield principles summarizing core mechanics.
14. **Learning Checks & Assessments:** Checkpoints with self-explanatory rationale and knowledge assessments with verified answer keys.

## 2. Three-Tier Content Depth Standards

Every chapter implements three distinct depth levels that can be toggled by the learner:

### Tier 1: Foundation (`foundation`)
- **Audience:** Motivated beginners, allied health learners, international students, and non-native English speakers.
- **Pedagogical Mandate:** Detailed, lucid, and thorough explanations without assuming prior specialized biochemical vocabulary.
- **Rules:**
  - Introduce every unfamiliar scientific term with a plain definition immediately upon first appearance.
  - Explain *HOW* and *WHY* processes happen, not just *WHAT* they are called.
  - Never limit Foundation sections to 1–2 short paragraphs. Provide substantive multi-paragraph explanations that build complete conceptual models.
  - Use clear, direct International English with short-to-medium sentences.
  - Provide intuitive physical analogies (e.g., comparing the respiratory quotient to a fuel gauge indicating whether an engine is burning gasoline or diesel).

### Tier 2: Academic (`academic`)
- **Audience:** University undergraduate medical, dietetics, nursing, and biomedical science students.
- **Pedagogical Mandate:** Rigorous, university-level biochemical and physiological depth.
- **Rules:**
  - Detail complete biochemical pathways, transporter names (SGLT1, GLUT5, PepT1, FAT/CD36, DMT1), hormones, and second messengers.
  - Provide chemical formulas, stoichiometric reactions, and rate-limiting regulatory steps.
  - Explain tissue compartmentation (mitochondrial matrix, cytosol, brush-border membrane, endoplasmic reticulum, portal circulation vs. lymphatic lacteals).
  - Integrate clinical nutritional biochemistry (e.g., how insulin resistance impairs lipoprotein lipase, or how hepcidin triggers ferroportin endocytosis and lysosomal degradation).

### Tier 3: Advanced / Research (`advanced`)
- **Audience:** Advanced clinical dietitians, physicians, academic researchers, and postgraduate clinicians.
- **Pedagogical Mandate:** Genuine advanced systems biology, clinical trial evidence evaluation, research methodologies, and therapeutic controversies.
- **Rules:**
  - Must include complete structured sections (minimum 6 sections matching or complementing Foundation and Academic).
  - Critically appraise landmark trials (e.g., PREDIMED, DiRECT, LOOK AHEAD, DASH-Sodium, KDIGO guidelines).
  - Address research methodology: stable isotope tracers, indirect calorimetry errors, hyperinsulinemic-euglycemic clamps, metagenomic 16S vs shotgun sequencing.
  - Rigorously differentiate established consensus guidelines from emerging evidence and unsupported claims.

## 3. Bilingual Standard & Quality (`বাংলা মানদণ্ড`)

Both English and Bengali are first-class pedagogical languages in this academy.

### Simple International English:
- Direct, active voice with clear transitions between paragraphs.
- Avoid idioms, colloquialisms, or unnecessarily ornate prose that confounds ESL readers.
- Explain the macroscopic clinical picture before descending into microscopic enzymology.

### Bengali Language Quality:
- Read as authoritative, elegant, natural popular science and academic prose.
- Pair English technical terms with Bengali on first mention:
  - *বেসাল মেটাবলিক রেট (Basal Metabolic Rate - BMR)*
  - *শর্ট-চেইন ফ্যাটি অ্যাসিড (Short-Chain Fatty Acids - SCFA)*
  - *ব্রাশ-বর্ডার হাইড্রোলেজ (Brush-border hydrolase)*
- Maintain strict conceptual and depth parity: every mechanism, equation, and qualification present in English must be fully explained in Bengali.
- Ensure culturally accessible dietary examples where appropriate (e.g., rice, lentils, mustard oil, fish) alongside international nutritional benchmarks.

## 4. Vancouver Numeric Citation Standard

1. **Inline Formatting:** Compact numeric brackets: `[1]`, `[2]`, `[1, 3]`.
2. **Strict Prohibition:** Never insert raw URLs, publisher strings, or bibliographic text within chapter prose.
3. **Sequential Numbering:** References are numbered in order of first appearance within the chapter.
4. **Source Reuse:** Re-cite existing numbers when referring to the same reference.
5. **Bidirectional Anchors:** Every citation links to `#ref-${chapterId}-${number}` with focus management, and every reference item includes an accessible return button to the reader's exact paragraph.
6. **Authoritative Sources Only:** Rely on peer-reviewed clinical trials, authoritative academic textbooks, and major guidelines (ADA, EASD, AHA, ESPEN, WHO, NIH). Never fabricate citations or DOIs.

## 5. Quantitative Worked Examples & Interactive Tools

Each chapter must include:
1. **At least 2 quantitative or clinical worked examples:**
   - Detailed step-by-step mathematical reasoning (e.g., Mifflin-St Jeor RMR calculation, DIAAS score computation, Weir equation derivation, electrolyte osmolarity, calcium-phosphate ion product).
   - Clear numerical values, units, formulas, and clinical conclusions.
2. **Interactive Checkpoints & Assessments:**
   - Conceptual self-checks that test reasoning rather than rote memory.
   - Verified multiple-choice questions with detailed physiological explanations for both correct and incorrect options.
3. **SVG & Interactive Visualizations:**
   - Every chapter must feature a custom interactive diagram (`InteractiveNutrientDiagram`) with clickable nodes, anatomical/metabolic coordinates, bilingual labels, and deep links to chapter sections.
